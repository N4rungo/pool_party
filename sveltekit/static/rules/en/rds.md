# 🎱 RDS — Runout Drill System

> Rack, break, and run the balls… just like playing regular pool.

## 🎯 Objective
RDS is a solo training system: a set of **16 break-and-run challenges** of
increasing difficulty, covering 8-ball, 9-ball, 10-ball, and straight pool
patterns. There is no dedicated setup — you just rack, break, and try to run
out under that level's rules.

## 🔢 How a rack works
- Break a rack of the size required by your current level (6 to 15 balls).
- There is no penalty for a scratch on the break, and any balls pocketed on
  the break stay down.
- After the break, you get **ball in hand (BIH)** — you may place the cue
  ball anywhere on the table — according to the rules of your level (every
  shot, a limited number of extra times, or just after the break).
- Try to pocket all the required balls without a miss or a foul, following
  the level's rules (any order, in rotation, or 8-ball groups).

## 📈 16 levels of difficulty

| # | Balls | Rules | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | Pocket directly, no cue ball | lower novice |
| 2 | 6 | Any order, BIH on every shot | mid novice |
| 3 | 6 | Any order, 3 extra BIHs | upper novice |
| 4 | 6 | Any order, 2 extra BIHs | lower beginner (D-) |
| 5 | 6 | Any order, 1 extra BIH | mid beginner (D) |
| 6 | 7 (3 solids, 3 stripes, 8) | 8-ball rules, 1 extra BIH | upper beginner (D+) |
| 7 | 9 | Any order, 1 extra BIH | lower intermediate (C-) |
| 8 | 9 (4 solids, 4 stripes, 8) | 8-ball rules, 1 extra BIH | mid intermediate (C) |
| 9 | 15 | Any order, 2 extra BIHs | upper intermediate (C+) |
| 10 | 6 | In order (rotation) | lower advanced (B-) |
| 11 | 15 | Any order | mid advanced (B) |
| 12 | 15 | 8-ball rules | upper advanced (B+) |
| 13 | 9 (4 solids, 4 stripes, 8) | 8-ball rules, remaining balls in order | lower shortstop (A-) |
| 14 | 9 | 9-ball rules | upper shortstop (A) |
| 15 | 15 | 8-ball rules, remaining balls in order | semipro / pro (A+/AA) |
| 16 | 15 | In order (rotation) | world class pro (A++/AAA) |

## 🔄 Progression
RDS is **progressive and adaptive**:
- Run **2 out of 3** racks at a level (all required balls, no miss or foul)
  → you move up to the next level.
- Run only **1 out of 3** → you stay at the current level.
- Run **0 out of 3** → you drop to the next lower level.

Pick a level where you're confident you can run 2 out of 3 racks to start, or
resume from where you left off in your previous session. Your ending level
and rating are a good indicator of your playing ability — track it over time
to monitor your improvement.

## 🏆 Winning
There's no single "win" — RDS is a training loop. Each session ends whenever
you choose to stop, and your reached level (and best level ever reached) are
what you're building on session after session.
