# 🎯 Chicago
> First to 61 points — simple and effective.

## 🎯 Objective
Score **61 points before your opponent** by pocketing balls. Each ball is worth its number (the 8 is worth 8, the 15 is worth 15, etc.). Total points on the table: 1 + 2 + … + 15 = **120 points**, so 61 guarantees the majority.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 players**
- Standard rack of 15 balls, racked randomly.
- Coin toss to decide who breaks.

## ▶️ How to play
Like 9-ball, on your turn you must target the lowest-numbered ball on the table — but any ball can be legally pocketed as a result.
- ✅ You pocket a ball → its number is added to your score, you keep shooting.
- ❌ You miss → confirm the end of your turn in the app, play passes.

> ✏️ **Note**: on a foul, the opponent may (if they wish) place the cue ball behind the head string. If the lowest ball is in that zone, it is moved to the foot spot (or as close as possible).

## 🏆 Winning
The first player to reach **61 points** or more wins immediately. 🥳
If all balls are pocketed before anyone reaches 61, the player with the **highest score** wins. In case of a perfect tie: draw. 🤝

## 💡 Example — end of game
Léa is at 54 points, Tom at 47. Léa pockets the 9 → 63 points → **game won immediately**, even though Tom could have pocketed the 14 (worth more) on the next shot.
