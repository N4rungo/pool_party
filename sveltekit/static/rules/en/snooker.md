# 🎱 Snooker
> Maximum break: 147 — the great English classic.

## 🎯 Objective
Score **more points** than your opponents by pocketing balls in a precise order: alternating red / colour, then the colours in sequence.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 4 players**
- **15 red balls** (1 pt each) + **6 colours**:

| Ball | Points |
|---|---|
| 🟡 Yellow | 2 |
| 🟢 Green | 3 |
| 🟫 Brown | 4 |
| 🔵 Blue | 5 |
| 🌸 Pink | 6 |
| ⚫ Black | 7 |

- Game mode **Simple** or **Expert** (chosen at setup) — affects how fouls are handled.

## ▶️ How to play
Snooker is played in two phases.

### Phase 1 — Red / colour alternation
While reds remain on the table:
1. **Red** first (+1 pt). The pocketed red stays off the table.
2. **Colour** next (+ its points). The colour is **replaced on its spot** after each shot.
3. **Red** again, and so on.

You keep shooting as long as you pocket in the correct order. As soon as you miss or pocket out of order, turn passes.

### Phase 2 — Colours in order
Once all reds are pocketed, the colours are played **in ascending point order**: yellow → green → brown → blue → pink → black. This time, **colours are not replaced**: a pocketed colour is gone.

## ⚠️ Fouls
A foul awards points to the opponent (to all other players in a 3–4 player game).
The penalty is **at least 4 points**, or the value of the ball involved if higher (max 7 pts).

### 🟢 Simple mode
Penalty points are **automatically awarded** to the other players. The player who fouled passes their turn. Simple and fast.

### 🔴 Expert mode
The fouled player has a **real choice**:
- **Take the points** (as in simple mode), then play normally.
- **Force a replay**: the table stays as it was after the foul — a tactical option.
- **Free ball**: if the position makes it impossible to aim at a legal ball (snookered), any ball may be nominated as a free ball, worth the same points as the ball that should have been played.

## 🏆 Winning
When all balls are pocketed (or the other players concede because the score gap is unassailable), **highest score wins**. 🥳

## 💡 Example — a 35-point break
Léa pockets: red (+1), black (+7), red (+1), black (+7), red (+1), black (+7), red (+1), blue (+5), red (+1), blue (+5) → break of **35 points**.
She misses the next shot: play passes to Tom. But Léa keeps her 35 points.
