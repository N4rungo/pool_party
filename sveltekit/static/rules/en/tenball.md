# 🎱 10-Ball

> Call your shot, pocket the 10!

## 🎯 Objective
Legally pocket the **10 ball**. The first player to do so wins the game.
Every shot must be **called** (ball + pocket) before it is played.

## 🔢 Setup
The 10 balls are racked in a **triangle**:
- The **1 ball** at the front (closest to the breaker)
- The **10 ball** in the centre
- Balls 2–9 placed randomly

The cue ball is placed behind the **head string** by the breaking player.

## 🎱 The break
- The breaking player must **hit the 1 ball first**.
- A legal break requires either: a ball pocketed **or** at least **4 balls reaching a cushion**.
- If the **10 ball** is pocketed on the break: **instant win** for the breaker.
- If the **cue ball** is pocketed: **ball in hand** for the next player (behind the head string).
- Illegal break → the next player may demand a **re-rack** or play from where the balls rest.

## 📢 Called shots
Before **every shot** (including the break), the player must call the intended ball and pocket.
- A ball pocketed **without being called** does not count: it stays down, but the turn passes.
- A ball that drops **accidentally** (unintended deflection) does not count.

## ▶️ Taking a turn
- You must always **hit the lowest-numbered ball** on the table first.
- If you legally pocket the called ball, you keep shooting. Otherwise: **turn passes**.
- The **10 ball** can be pocketed at any time on a legal, called shot:
  directly, off a cushion, or by **combination** with the lowest ball.

## ⚠️ Fouls → ball in hand for the next player
A foul is committed if:
- You **pocket the cue ball** (scratch)
- You hit any ball other than the **lowest-numbered** one first
- After contact, no ball touches a **cushion** and no ball is pocketed
- The cue ball or any ball **leaves the table**

On a foul, the next player gets **ball in hand** anywhere on the table.

## 👥 Multiplayer
Players compete in a **set order** established at the start.
Each player keeps shooting as long as they legally pocket their called balls.
On a miss, foul, or uncalled pocket, play passes to the **next player in order**.

## 🏆 Winning
Legally pocket the **10 ball** with a called shot — at any point during the game.

---

## 🧠 Expert rule: Push out
Immediately after the break, the breaking player may call a **push out**:
they play the cue ball freely, with no obligation to hit the lowest ball
or make rail contact. The 10 ball cannot be pocketed on a push out.

The next player may then:
- **Accept the position** and play normally
- **Force the breaker** to replay from that position
