# 📏 14-1 Continuous
> Count your points, not your balls.

## 🎯 Objective
Reach a **target score** before the others by pocketing balls continuously.
Each ball pocketed = **1 point**, regardless of its number.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 6 players**
- Shared target score (default **50 points**, configurable from 10 to 300 in steps of 10)
- Score adjustable per player at step 3 (handicap to mix beginners and experienced players)
- Standard rack of 15 balls

## ▶️ How to play
14-1 is played in **runs**: on your turn, you pocket as many balls in a row as you can.
- ✅ You pocket a ball → +1 to your current run, you keep shooting.
- ❌ You miss → confirm the end of your run in the app: your score increases accordingly, turn passes.
- ⚠️ Foul → you can declare a foul in the app (turn passes; any penalty is up to your house rules).

### 🔁 The "continuous" rule
When only **1 ball remains** on the table: **re-rack the other 14**, leave the last ball in place, break through the new rack, and keep going. This is what allows scoring indefinitely until the target is reached.

## 🏆 Winning
The first player to reach **their** target score wins. 🥳

## 💡 Example — a great run
Tom is at 12 points, his target is 50. He pockets 6 balls in a row then misses.
He confirms → run of 6, total score 18. Play passes to Léa. Tom will need one or two more big runs to win.
