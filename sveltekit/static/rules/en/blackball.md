# 🎱 Blackball

> Pot your balls, then the black.

## 🎯 Objective
Two players (or teams) compete. One plays **yellows**, the other plays **reds**.
The first to pot all 7 of their balls and then the **black ball** wins the game.

## 🟡🔴 Ball groups
| Group | Balls |
|-------|-------|
| **Yellows** | 7 yellow balls |
| **Reds** | 7 red balls |
| **The black** | 1 black ball — key to victory |

Groups are determined by the **first ball legally pocketed during normal play**
(not on the break). If a ball is pocketed on the break, the player continues but
groups remain unassigned.

## 🎱 The break
- The breaking player shoots from the **D** (baulk semi-circle).
- If the **black ball** is pocketed on the break: **re-rack**.
- If the **cue ball** is pocketed: ball in hand in the D for the opponent.

## ▶️ Taking a turn
- You must **hit one of your own balls first**.
- You keep shooting as long as you pocket **one of your own balls**.
- If you miss, pocket an opponent's ball, or commit a foul: **turn passes**.

## ⚠️ Fouls → 2 visits for the opponent
A foul is committed if:
- You **pocket the cue ball**
- You hit **an opponent's ball or the black** first (before clearing your own)
- You **don't hit any ball**
- The cue ball or any ball **leaves the table**

On a foul, the opponent earns **2 visits** (two turns at the table).
The first visit may be played defensively (intentionally).
If the player pots all their remaining balls on the first visit, the 2nd is forfeit.

**Ball in hand:** if the cue ball was pocketed or left the table,
the opponent places the cue ball anywhere within the **D**.

## 🏆 Win on foul
If a player only has the black ball left and their opponent commits a foul,
they may **claim an immediate win**.

## 👥 Team play
2 variants are possible:
- Teammates alternate after each ball pocketed.
- The same player stays at the table as long as they keep pocketing balls.

## ⚫ The black ball
The black ball may only be played **after pocketing all 7 of your balls**.
The intended pocket must be **called** before the shot.

## ❌ Instant loss
You lose immediately if:
- You pocket the **black ball before** your 7 balls
- You pocket the **black ball and cue ball** at the same time
- You **knock the black ball off** the table

## 🏆 Winning
Pot the black ball at the right moment, in the called pocket — **game over**.
