# 🎱 8-Ball
> Pocket your 7 balls, then the black.

## 🎯 Objective
Two players (or teams) compete. One plays **solids** (balls 1–7), the other plays **stripes** (balls 9–15). The first to pocket all their balls and then the **8 ball** wins the game.

## 🔵🔴 Ball groups
| Group | Balls |
|-------|-------|
| **Solids** | 1 to 7 — solid colours |
| **Stripes** | 9 to 15 — striped |
| **The black** | 8 — key to victory |

Groups are determined by the **first ball pocketed after the break**, regardless of who pockets it.

## 🔢 Setup
The 15 balls are racked in a **triangle** at the foot of the table:
- The **8 ball** goes in the **centre** of the triangle, on the **foot spot**.
- The **back two corners** of the triangle must each hold one **solid** and one **stripe** — never two balls of the same group.
- The **front ball** (apex) can be either a solid or a stripe.
- All other balls are placed randomly.

The cue ball is placed anywhere behind the **head string** by the breaking player.

## 🎱 The break
- The breaking player must strike the rack and **spread the balls**.
- If a ball drops, they continue their turn. Otherwise, the opponent plays from where the balls came to rest.
- If the **8 ball** is pocketed on the break: **re-rack**.
- If the **cue ball** is pocketed on the break: **ball in hand** for the opponent (behind the head string).

## ▶️ Taking a turn
- You must **hit one of your own balls first**.
- You keep shooting as long as you pocket **one of your own balls**.
- If you miss, pocket an opponent's ball, or commit a foul: **turn passes**.

## ⚠️ Fouls → ball in hand for the opponent
A foul is committed if:
- You **pocket the cue ball** (scratch)
- You hit **an opponent's ball first** (or the 8 ball before clearing your own)
- You **don't hit any ball**
- After contact, no ball touches a **cushion** and no ball is pocketed

On a foul, the opponent has **ball in hand**: they can place the cue ball anywhere on the table before shooting.

## 👥 Team play
2 variants are possible:
- Teammates alternate after each ball pocketed.
- The same player stays at the table as long as they keep pocketing balls.

## ⚫ The 8 ball
The 8 ball may only be played **after pocketing all your own balls**. The intended pocket must be **called** before the shot.

## ❌ Instant loss
You lose immediately if:
- You pocket the **8 ball before** your 7 balls
- You pocket the **8 ball and the cue ball** at the same time
- You **knock the 8 ball off** the table

## 🏆 Winning
Pocket the 8 ball at the right moment, in the called pocket — **game over**.
