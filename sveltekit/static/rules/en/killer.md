# 🎱 Killer
> Be the last one standing.

## 🎯 Objective
Each player starts with a set number of lives. You lose one every time you miss a shot.
Last player alive wins.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 15 players**
- **1 to 5 lives** per player (3 by default)
- Turn order is drawn at random

## ▶️ How to play
On your turn, you must **pocket a ball** (any ball).
- ✅ Success → turn passes, no life lost.
- ❌ Miss → you lose 1 life ❤️.
- ⚫ 8 ball pocketed → **+1 life** (capped at 5).
- 💀 No lives left → you are eliminated.

### 🔄 No balls left on the table?
Re-rack, break, and carry on. The game resumes where it left off.

## 🃏 Jokers
Each player can use **up to 3 jokers** per game.

| Icon | Joker | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| ⏭️ | **Skip your turn** | Play passes to the next player without you shooting. |
| ✋ | **Ball in hand** | Place the cue ball wherever you like before your shot. |
| 🎯 | **Pick a player** | The chosen player must take a forced turn immediately (without being able to use a joker). |

### Two modes (chosen at game creation)
- **🎲 Random** — A shared joker pool; one is drawn at random when a player asks to use one.
- **🃏 Free choice** — Each player starts with 1 of each type, to be used whenever they want.

## 🏆 Winning
The last player still alive wins the game. 🥳

## 💡 Example — the forced turn
Léa has one life left. It's Tom's turn. Tom plays his 🎯 joker and picks Léa: she must shoot **immediately**, without being able to use a joker. If she misses, she loses her last life and is eliminated.
The game then resumes its normal course.
