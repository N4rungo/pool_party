# 🌀 Casin
> French billiards, remixed as a checklist.

## 🎯 Objective
Complete **9 different actions**, each a set number of times.
The first player to tick off their entire checklist wins.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 6 players**
- A **French billiards** variant (3 balls: 2 white/yellow + 1 red)
- Number of repetitions per action **X** (default **2**, configurable globally from 1 to 5, and adjustable per player)
- The cue ball alternates (white / yellow) between players each turn.

## 📋 The 9 actions

| Icon | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 🎯 | **Direct** | Hit both balls without touching any cushion. |
| 🔓 | **Free** | No restrictions (standard cannon). |
| 🔴 | **Red** | Hit the red ball first. |
| 1️⃣ | **1 rail** | 1 cushion before hitting the 2nd ball. |
| 2️⃣ | **2 rails** | 2 cushions before hitting the 2nd ball. |
| 3️⃣ | **3 rails** | 3 or more cushions before hitting the 2nd ball. |
| ↩️ | **Rail first** | At least 1 cushion **before** hitting the 1st ball. |
| ✋ | **Weak hand** | Legal shot played with the non-dominant hand. |
| 🌀 | **Casin** | Hit the red, which then touches the 2nd ball via at least 1 cushion. |

## ▶️ How to play
On your turn, you play as in French billiards.
- 🗣️ **Before your shot**: announce the action you intend to perform.
- ✅ **Action succeeded** → tick one box on that action, shoot again. **You cannot announce the same action on your next shot**: you must choose a different one.
- 🎱 **Action failed but cannon was legal** (you did hit both balls) → nothing ticked, but you **shoot again** and may call any action you like.
- ❌ **No cannon** → turn passes.

## 🏆 Winning
The first player to have ticked **all actions X times** wins. 🥳

## 💡 Example — the "not the same twice" rule
Tom calls **Direct** and succeeds. He shoots again.
On the next shot, he **cannot** call Direct again (even if he hasn't ticked it X times yet). He picks **1 rail**, for example.
- If he misses 1 rail but still hits both balls → he continues, may try something else next.
- If he also misses the cannon → turn passes.
