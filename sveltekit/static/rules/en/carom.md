# 🎱 Carom
> Strike both object balls with a single shot.

## 🎯 Objective
Reach a **target score** by making successful caroms.
A carom = the cue ball touches **the red AND the yellow** (or spot-white) in a single shot → **1 point**.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 6 players**
- 3 balls on the table: the **white** (cue ball), the **red** and the **yellow** (or spot-white)
- Target score is configurable and can be set individually per player (handicap to balance skill levels)

## ▶️ How to play
Carom is played in **runs**: on your turn, make as many caroms in a row as you can.
- ✅ Successful carom → +1 to your current run, keep shooting.
- ❌ Missed shot → your run ends, play passes to the next player.

### ⚠️ No penalty for fouls
In classic carom billiards, an irregular shot carries no point penalty — the turn simply passes.

## 🎮 Disciplines
Carom comes in several variants depending on the constraints placed on the cue ball:

- **Straight rail** (Libre) — no constraints; the cue ball may contact the balls in any order and from any angle.
- **One-cushion** — the cue ball must contact at least 1 cushion before completing the carom.
- **Three-cushion** — the cue ball must contact at least 3 cushions before completing the carom. The premier discipline of carom billiards.

## 🏆 Winning
The first player to reach **their** target score wins. 🥳
