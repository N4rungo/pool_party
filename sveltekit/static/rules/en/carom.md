# 🎱 Carom Billiards
> Hit both object balls with one shot.

## 🎯 Objective
Reach a **target score** before the others by making successful caroms.
A carom = the cue ball touches **both other balls** in a single shot → **1 point**.

## 🎱 Setup
- **2 to 6 players**
- Shared target score (default **20 points**, configurable from 5 to 200 in steps of 5)
- Score adjustable per player at step 3 (handicap to mix skill levels)
- French billiard table with 3 balls: 1 white (cue ball), 1 red, 1 white (opponent)

## ▶️ How to play
Carom is played in **runs**: on your turn, make as many caroms in a row as you can.
- ✅ You make a carom (cue ball touches both other balls) → +1 to your current run, keep shooting.
- ❌ You miss → confirm the end of your run in the app: your score increases accordingly, turn passes.

### ⚠️ No fouls
In classic carom billiards, there is no penalty for a foul. A missed shot simply ends your turn.

## 🏆 Winning
The first player to reach **their** target score wins. 🥳

## 💡 Example
Julie is at 8 points, her target is 20. She makes 5 caroms in a row then misses.
She confirms → run of 5, total score 13. Play passes to Marc.
